# v0.1.0 beta — concept brief

Original planning ID: 001. The first release is now named **v0.1.0 beta**.
Status: first local UI simulation built. Live integrations and custom agent configuration remain deferred.
Updated: 2026-09-18.

The current release is **v0.1.11**. This document preserves the original `v0.1.0-NN` ticket IDs as historical implementation records. The next routine patch is **v0.1.12**.

## Confirmed by the owner

- A digital TCG gacha web app backed by physical stock, mostly Lorcana booster packs and graded cards.
- Three machine tiers: Common, Rare, Epic.
- Higher machine tiers cost more and can award better, more expensive items.
- The owner will supply the items in each machine and prices. Exact pools and prices are pending.
- After receiving an item, a customer can resell immediately at a percentage, keep it in digital inventory, or request physical shipping.
- Payments are crypto-only for v0.1.0 beta. The resale settlement asset and destination are not yet specified.
- Physical stock is stored in Portugal for this release.
- The audience is TCG fans, especially the Lorcana community. Candidate marketing channels are X, Instagram, TikTok, and possibly Facebook.
- The experience should feel like a retro pixel game and load quickly. The owner has original handmade pixel artwork and animations.
- Work should use small numbered tickets and models appropriate to each task.
- The working name is Gacha Arcade, confirmed by the owner.
- The current build is explicitly a mockup and simulation. Real prices and wallet connection will be added later.
- The whole interface must consistently use pixel fonts, pixel icons, and colors matching the original artwork.
- The owner supplied `https://github.com/kairo8080/gacha` and selected Vercel as the deployment direction. A custom domain has not been purchased.

## Decisions still open

### Resale contract — active discussion

- What the percentage represents: a payout percentage or a deducted fee.
- The base value: for example, a published item value or another explicitly defined amount.
- The percentage, and whether it varies by item or machine.
- Whether resale pays crypto to an external wallet, creates a withdrawable balance, or grants spendable site credit.
- How long an immediate resale offer lasts, and whether resale is also available later from digital inventory.
- Whether a resold item returns to the same machine, another pool, or unallocated stock.

### Crypto payments

- Network and accepted payment token(s).
- Wallet/account access, payment confirmation policy, transaction fees, and handling of failed or late payments.
- Whether a purchase pays directly for a pull or first funds an account balance.

### Availability and fulfillment

- Customer purchase countries, shipping destinations, and minimum age. Portugal is the storage location; it does not establish these policies or the operating entity's jurisdiction.
- Shipping prices, combined shipments, storage duration, and who handles fulfillment.
- Exact physical unit awarded: sealed pack, graded card, bundle, or another unit. Opening a booster to award its individual contents has not been specified.

### Machine pools

- Prize selection method, disclosed odds, stock depletion, and replenishment rules.
- Whether stock is allocated exclusively to one machine or shared across machines.
- Item valuations, acquisition costs, and resale funding needed to assess machine economics.
- Common/Rare/Epic are machine tiers; their relationship to printed TCG rarity remains to be defined.

## Proposed correctness requirements — for review

- An item held in digital inventory remains backed by physical stock reserved for that customer.
- Every graded card is tracked by its exact physical identity, including grade and certification where available.
- An item cannot be resold and shipped at the same time. Request retries must not create a second award or resale payout.
- Payment, draw outcome, ownership, and resale settlement have durable records that can be reconciled after interruptions.
- The recorded award determines the reveal. Reloading or skipping an animation must recover the same award.
- A resale quote shows the valuation basis, percentage, resulting amount, settlement asset, and applicable fees before confirmation.
- A shipping request locks the selected inventory while it is being fulfilled; cancellation rules remain to be defined.

These are proposed system properties, not a selected implementation or a claim of publicly verifiable fairness.

## Technical direction — provisional

- Next.js and TypeScript for public pages and interactive machine screens.
- Supabase/Postgres for inventory, accounts, purchases, ownership, and fulfillment.
- Vercel as the owner's current deployment choice, with the Next.js preset; Railway was the initial candidate.
- Prerender suitable public content and keep machine interaction lightweight; protect personalized data and recheck live availability during purchase.
- Preserve original pixel assets and define their scaling, animation, and loading rules after an asset review.
- Crypto-only payments are confirmed. On-chain randomness, tokenized ownership, NFTs, and additional blockchain features are not yet specified.
